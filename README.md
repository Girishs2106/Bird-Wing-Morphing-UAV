# Bird Wing Morphing UAV
**Bio-Inspired Aircraft Design | Mechanical Design | CAD Modelling**

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on the mechanical design and CAD modelling of a bird-inspired morphing wing UAV. The concept explores how bird wing structures and movements can inspire adaptable aircraft wing configurations.

The project uses SolidWorks to develop the wing geometry, mechanical arrangement, and 3D assembly.

## 2. Problem Statement

Conventional UAVs generally use fixed wing configurations. Birds, however, change their wing shape and position to adapt to different flight conditions.

This project explores a bird-inspired wing design to understand how morphing mechanisms can provide adaptable wing configurations.

## 3. Objectives

- Develop a bird-inspired morphing wing concept.
- Create 3D CAD models using SolidWorks.
- Explore mechanical arrangements for wing movement.
- Study wing geometry and structural design.
- Understand the challenges involved in manufacturing morphing wing structures.

## 4. Mechanical Design

The design focuses on a wing structure inspired by bird anatomy, with attention to wing geometry, mechanical movement, and structural arrangement.

The CAD development includes wing components, structural elements, and their assembly.
